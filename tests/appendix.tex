\section{Additional Analysis}

In this appendix, we provide additional analysis of the game-theoretic approach \cite{qian2022game} discussed in the main paper.

\subsection{Theoretical Background}

The D2D collaborative offloading scheme proposed by Qian et al. \cite{qian2022game} is based on non-cooperative game theory. This approach ensures Nash equilibrium in the resource allocation problem.

\subsection{Comparison with DRL Methods}

Compared to deep reinforcement learning methods \cite{he2017integrated}, game-theoretic approaches offer better interpretability but may have limitations in dynamic environments.
